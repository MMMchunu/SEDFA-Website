TREP is Now Enhanced
Township and Rural Entrepreneurship Programme - TREP
Overview

The Township and Rural Entrepreneurship Programme (TREP) is a DSBD programme implemented through its agency, SEDFA, to support township and rural enterprises with affordable finance, blended finance options and business development support to help MSMEs grow sustainably.

Apply Now
Download Brochure
Priority is given to businesses owned by women, youth and persons with disabilities.
What's New in TREP?

  • Funding of up to R3 million per business.

  • The programme offers tiered fixed-rate debt pricing, set at 5% fixed for loan amounts up to R1 million and 8% fixed on facilities exceeding R1 million up to the R3 million ceiling.

  • Repayment terms of up to 60 months.

  • Moratorium of up to 6 months on capital and interest, based on your cashflow.

  • Blended finance: 60% loan and a grant of up to 40%, determined by SEDFA’s Grant Scorecard.

  • Open to all sectors — including digital, green economy, creative and tourism.

  • Business development and post-investment support.

Am I Eligible?

  • Registered with CIPC (including co-operatives and sole proprietors)

  • 100% South African-owned

  • Operating in a township or rural area

  • Registered with SARS

  • Valid business bank account

  • At least one owner actively involved in running the business
