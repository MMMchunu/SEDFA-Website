Learning Academy

Overview

The SEDFA Learning Academy is an accredited education and training provider dedicated to developing the skills, knowledge, and expertise of Small Business Development Support Practitioners.

By enhancing practitioner competence and professionalism, the Academy contributes to improved service delivery to small enterprises and supports sustainable economic growth.

As a central platform for Business Development Services (BDS), the Academy provides:

  • Practitioner training
  • Partner coordination
  • Certification
  • Quality assurance
  • Small enterprise training and development

Why Choose Us?
  • Accredited training provider
  • Industry-focused learning programmes
  • Professional practitioner development
  • Partnerships with leading institutions and experts
  • Practical exposure and mentorship opportunities

Our Training Offerings

The Academy provides programmes based on identified sector stakeholder needs:

  • Accredited Programmes and Non-Credit Bearing Programmes
  • Short Skills Programmes
  • Qualifications
  • Industry Exposure Capacity Building Programmes
  • Internships and Learnerships
Flexible Learning Methods

Training is delivered through:

  • In-house Trainers
  • External Business Development Service Providers
  • Educational Institutions
  • Professional Body Partnerships
  • International Technical Advisors

Learning Formats

Our programmes incorporate:

  • Lectures & Structured Learning
  • Seminars & Webinars
  • Virtual & Online Learning
  • Mentorship & Coaching

Training Registration

External Practitioners

  • Register through the SEDFA website or official SEDFA social media platforms when programmes are advertised.
  • Partner institutions recruit and submit participant lists
  • Provincial networks conduct recruitment and selection processes.

Small Enterprises

  • Recruitment and selection are conducted jointly by provincial networks and the Learning Academy.
  • Registration opportunities are advertised on SEDFA social media platforms
  • Funding organisations may recruit and submit participants directly to the Academy.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)



What is the SEDFA Learning Academy?

An accredited provider of education and training responsible for the training and capacity building of Small Business Development Support Practitioners so that requisite skills, knowledge and relevant expertise are acquired by the participants thus improving the quality of services being delivered to small enterprises.

What programmes does the Learning Academy offer?

The Learning Academy offers:

  • Accredited Programmes
  • Non-Credit Bearing Programmes
  • Short Skills Programmes
  • Industry Exposure Capacity Building Programmes
  • Externally funded Internships
  • Externally funded Learnerships
How do I register for a programme?

Registration is done once a training opportunity is advertised. Internal practitioners apply through internal communication channels, while external practitioners and small enterprises register through SEDFA communication platforms, provincial networks, or participating institutions.

How are applications evaluated?

All applications are reviewed by the Learning Academy against programme requirements and participant eligibility criteria. Following evaluation, applicants are notified of the outcome.

How will I know if my application was successful?

Applications open on 13 May 2026 and close on 24 June 2026. Submissions can be done through the SEDFA website.  

What learning methods are used?

Training is delivered through a variety of methods, including:

  • Facilitation
  • Seminars and webinars
  • Virtual and online learning
  • Mentorship and coaching
  • Best-practice exposure sessions
  • Structured learning exercises and assignments
Does the Learning Academy offer online training?

Yes. Many programmes are delivered through virtual and online platforms, webinars, and facilitator-led digital learning sessions, allowing participants to learn remotely.

Are the programmes accredited?

Some programmes are accredited and aligned with National Qualifications Framework (NQF) standards and relevant quality assurance bodies. The Academy also offers non-credit bearing programmes designed to address specific industry and skills needs.

Does the Learning Academy provide training for small business owners?

TYes. The Learning Academy provides training and capacity-building programmes specifically designed for entrepreneurs and small enterprises, covering areas such as business management, funding readiness, digital business, compliance, and sector-specific skills

How can I stay informed about upcoming training opportunities?

Upcoming training opportunities are advertised through SEDFA communication platforms, social media channels, stakeholder networks, institutional partners, and internal communication channels for employees. Regularly visiting the Learning Academy webpage and following SEDFA communications will ensure that you do not miss new opportunities.

Do participants receive both a Certificate of Attendance and a Certificate of Competency upon completion of the training?

Yes, participants may receive either or both certificates, depending on whether they meet the applicable requirements:

  • Certificate of Attendance: Issued to participants who meet the required attendance criteria and successfully complete the training programme.
  • Certificate of Competency: Issued to participants who meet the attendance requirements and successfully pass all required assessments, thereby demonstrating competence in the learning outcomes of the training programme.
